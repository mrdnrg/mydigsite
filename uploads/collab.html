About the project

DIGITS is a collection of 999 number characters on Ethereum. The twist: your token ID is your number. Mint #1 is literally THE 1. First come, lowest number — so the mint itself is a race.

Every digit is a dressed-up character — streetwear, history, pop culture. Hidden across the 999 are special numbers (67, 300, 360, 737, 911…) drawn on their own theme, with the story in the description. Low numbers, meme numbers and specials are the chase.
